Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how kidscartwon ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our application, which automatically publishes video content ("Reels") to Instagram, Facebook, and Threads on behalf of the account owner.

1. Who We Are

kidscartwon is a personal/small content publishing tool used to schedule and publish short video content to social media platforms owned by Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads). It is operated by the app owner and is not a public-facing consumer product.

2. Information We Collect

To provide automatic publishing, our application accesses and processes:

  • Facebook Page ID and Page Access Token
  • Instagram Business Account ID
  • Threads User ID and Threads Access Token
  • Video files uploaded for publishing, and any captions/titles generated for them

We do not collect information from end users who view the published content. We only process the credentials and content belonging to the account owner who configured the application.

3. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate with the Meta Graph API and Threads API
  • To upload and publish video content (Reels/Threads posts) to the connected accounts
  • To generate captions/titles for published content using a third-party AI service

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage

Access tokens and configuration values are stored locally in a configuration file on the device running the application and are not transmitted to any server other than the official Meta Graph API, Threads API, and the video hosting service used to stage uploads before publishing.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Access tokens remain valid until they expire or are manually revoked by the account owner. You can revoke access at any time via your Meta or Threads account settings, or by deleting the local configuration file. Uploaded video files are removed from temporary hosting shortly after publishing completes.

6. Third-Party Services

This application interacts with the following third-party services to function:

  • Meta Graph API (Facebook, Instagram)
  • Threads API
  • A cloud media hosting service, used only to stage video files for publishing
  • An AI text-generation service, used only to generate captions/titles

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

7. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of any data associated with this application, contact us using the email address below. We will remove locally stored tokens and configuration data associated with your account upon request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
